# Himsagar Mango Market Launch Strategy for BigBasket Hyderabad

## Project Overview
This project is a business analytics and product launch case study focused on evaluating the opportunity to introduce Kolkata Himsagar mangoes in Hyderabad through BigBasket.

The objective was to assess whether a short-season, premium Bengali mango could become a profitable pilot product in selected Hyderabad micro-markets such as Nizampet and Gachibowli.

The project combines market sizing, demand estimation, unit economics, profitability modeling, customer segmentation, KPI planning, and executive presentation design.

## Business Problem
BigBasket operates in a highly competitive grocery and quick-commerce market where seasonal, regional, and premium products can improve customer engagement.

The key business question was:

**Can BigBasket profitably launch Kolkata Himsagar mangoes in Hyderabad by targeting Bengali households and premium fruit buyers?**

## Key Objectives
- Estimate seasonal demand potential in selected Hyderabad micro-markets
- Identify Bengali and premium non-Bengali customer segments
- Build a kg-based profitability model
- Estimate pilot-level and scale-up profit
- Recommend launch strategy, pricing, and KPI tracking
- Create an executive-ready presentation suitable for business stakeholders

## Key Assumptions
- Target micro-markets: Nizampet and Gachibowli
- Estimated app users in target areas: 1.4 lakh
- Bengali app users: 0.26 lakh
- Premium non-Bengali users: 1.10 lakh
- Pilot batch size: 2,500 kg
- Selling price: ₹310/kg
- Blended procurement cost: ₹181/kg
- Logistics and packaging cost: ₹35/kg
- Wastage assumption: 8%

## Key Business Insights
- Seasonal demand potential is estimated at 20,000–25,000 kg.
- A controlled 2,500 kg pilot can generate an estimated profit of ₹1.43 lakh.
- A 25,000 kg scale-up scenario can generate an estimated profit of ₹15.13 lakh.
- Premium non-Bengali buyers are a key growth driver beyond the Bengali diaspora segment.
- Wastage control, repeat purchase rate, and conversion rate are critical success metrics.

## Predicted Profitability

|---|---:|---:|
| Pilot Launch | 2,500 kg | ₹1.43 lakh |

## Recommended Pilot Strategy
Launch a 2,500 kg pilot in Nizampet and Gachibowli with targeted app promotions.

Recommended targeting:
- Bengali households
- Mango buyers
- Fish buyers as a Bengali food preference proxy
- Premium fruit buyers
- High-value grocery customers

## KPI Dashboard Metrics
The pilot should be tracked using:

- Conversion rate: 12% target
- Repeat purchase rate: 25% target
- Wastage: below 8%
- Average order value
- Add-to-cart rate
- Sell-through rate
- Profit per kg
- Customer cohort response
